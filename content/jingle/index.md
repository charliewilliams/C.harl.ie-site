## Features

### Real physics, real sound
Each instrument is a physical model, not a set of samples. The motion sensors in your phone drive the simulation, so a gentle tilt sounds very different from a hard shake.

### A box of shakers
Tambourine, maracas, egg shaker, sleigh bells and more, each with its own feel.

### Made for playing along
Pick up your phone and join in with the band, the radio or the kids.

### No accounts, no tracking
Jingle+ doesn't ask you to sign up and doesn't collect any data.

## Support

### Contact
Questions, bug reports or ideas: email [c@harl.ie](mailto:c@harl.ie?subject=Jingle%2B%20support). I read everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

### I can't hear anything
Check that your phone isn't on silent (the switch or Action button on the side) and that the volume is up. If you're using Bluetooth headphones, make sure they're connected.

### The sound is delayed over Bluetooth
Bluetooth audio adds latency, and the phone can't avoid it. For the tightest feel, use the built-in speaker or wired headphones.

### The app isn't responding to movement
Force-quit Jingle+ and open it again. If that doesn't fix it, restart your phone. If the problem keeps happening, email me with your iPhone model and iOS version.

### Restoring a purchase
Jingle+ is a paid download, so it's tied to your Apple Account. Re-download it from the App Store while signed in with the same account; you won't be charged again.

## Privacy

Jingle+ doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. The motion sensors are used only on your device to generate sound, and that data never leaves your phone.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships. Questions: [c@harl.ie](mailto:c@harl.ie).
